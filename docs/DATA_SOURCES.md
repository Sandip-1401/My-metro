# Verified Data Sources & Limitations

## 1. Authoritative Sources
- **Official Portal:** Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) — [https://www.gujaratmetrorail.com/ahmedabad/](https://www.gujaratmetrorail.com/ahmedabad/)
- **Network Scope:** Phase 1 Operational Corridors:
  - East-West Corridor (Blue Line): Thaltej Gam to Vastral Gam (~21.16 km, 18 stations)
  - North-South Corridor (Red Line): APMC to Motera Stadium (~18.87 km, 15 stations)
  - Key Interchange: Old High Court
- **Verification Date:** October 2026

## 2. Fare Schedule (Published Distance Slabs)
- 0 to 2 km: ₹5
- 2 to 5 km: ₹10
- 5 to 8 km: ₹15
- 8 to 12 km: ₹20
- Above 12 km: ₹25

## 3. Data Limitations & Simulation Boundaries
- **No Live Train GPS:** GMRC does not expose a real-time public GPS tracking API. Train timings are based on published operational timetables (approx. 6:20 AM to 10:00 PM, 7 to 15 min headways).
- **Demonstration Ticketing:** QR codes and tickets generated in this application are strictly demonstration tokens for portfolio and testing purposes, and are **not valid for actual metro travel**.
